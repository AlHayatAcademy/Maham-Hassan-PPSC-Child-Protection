# Test 16 - Pakistan: history, political firsts and constitutional milestones
Who was the first Prime Minister of Pakistan?|Liaquat Ali Khan|Khawaja Nazimuddin|Iskander Mirza|Muhammad Ali Bogra|Liaquat Ali Khan served from August 1947 until his assassination at Company Bagh, Rawalpindi on 16 October 1951. The park was later renamed Liaquat Bagh.
Who was the first President of Pakistan?|Iskander Mirza|Ayub Khan|Ghulam Muhammad|Yahya Khan|Iskander Mirza became the first President under the 1956 Constitution; before that the head of state was the Governor-General.
Who was the first Governor-General of Pakistan?|Muhammad Ali Jinnah|Khawaja Nazimuddin|Ghulam Muhammad|Iskander Mirza|Jinnah took the oath on 14-15 August 1947 and also was the first President of the Constituent Assembly.
Who was the first Finance Minister of Pakistan?|Malik Ghulam Muhammad|Chaudhry Muhammad Ali|Liaquat Ali Khan|Zafarullah Khan|Ghulam Muhammad later became Governor-General (1951-55). Ch. Muhammad Ali was Secretary-General and later PM.
Who was the first Foreign Minister of Pakistan?|Sir Zafarullah Khan|Liaquat Ali Khan|Hamid Nasir|Sardar Abdur Rab Nishtar|Zafarullah Khan represented Pakistan at the UN and later became a judge at the ICJ.
Who was the first Pakistani Commander-in-Chief of the Pakistan Army?|General Muhammad Ayub Khan|General Sir Frank Messervy|General Yahya Khan|General Gul Hassan|Ayub Khan took over in January 1951, succeeding the British General Douglas Gracey.
Who was the first Governor of the State Bank of Pakistan?|Zahid Hussain|Aziz Ahmad|Ghulam Muhammad|Muhammad Ali|The SBP began operations on 1 July 1948; Quaid-e-Azam inaugurated it on that date.
Who was the first Chairman of the Senate of Pakistan?|Habibullah Khan Marwat|Wasim Sajjad|Muhammad Mian Soomro|Nayyar Bokhari|The Senate was formed under the 1973 Constitution. Habibullah Khan was its first Chairman.
Who was the first woman Speaker of the National Assembly of Pakistan?|Dr Fehmida Mirza|Benazir Bhutto|Asma Jahangir|Nasim Wali Khan|She served 2008-2013.
The Objectives Resolution was passed on:|12 March 1949|23 March 1940|14 August 1947|10 April 1973|It was moved by Liaquat Ali Khan. It later became a substantive part of the Constitution via Article 2A.
The first Martial Law in Pakistan was imposed on:|7 October 1958|5 July 1977|12 October 1999|25 March 1969|President Iskander Mirza abrogated the 1956 Constitution; Ayub Khan became Chief Martial Law Administrator and removed Mirza within 20 days.
General Zia-ul-Haq imposed Martial Law on:|5 July 1977|7 October 1958|12 October 1999|25 March 1969|It was called Operation Fair Play and ousted Z.A. Bhutto.
General Pervez Musharraf took power on:|12 October 1999|5 July 1977|7 October 1958|25 March 1969|He ousted Nawaz Sharif's government after the Kargil conflict.
The first general election in Pakistan on the basis of adult franchise was held in:|December 1970|1956|1962|1977|The Awami League won 160 of 162 seats in East Pakistan; the PPP won 81 in West Pakistan.
Which party won an absolute majority in the 1970 elections?|Awami League|Pakistan Peoples Party|Muslim League|Jamaat-e-Islami|Despite its majority, power was not transferred, which led to the 1971 crisis.
The 1971 war ended on:|16 December 1971|14 August 1971|26 March 1971|3 December 1971|Dhaka fell on 16 December. The Hamoodur Rahman Commission inquired into the debacle.
The Agartala Conspiracy Case (1968) was linked to:|Sheikh Mujibur Rahman|Z.A. Bhutto|Wali Khan|Ayub Khan|The case alleged a conspiracy with India to separate East Pakistan. It was withdrawn in 1969.
The Six Points of Sheikh Mujib were presented in:|1966 at Lahore|1947|1971|1956|They demanded provincial autonomy and were presented at a conference of opposition parties in Lahore.
The Tashkent Declaration was signed in:|January 1966|September 1965|July 1972|February 1999|It ended the 1965 war and was mediated by the Soviet Union.
The Lahore Declaration was signed by Nawaz Sharif and Atal Bihari Vajpayee in:|February 1999|1972|1966|2001|It was followed by the Kargil conflict in May-July 1999.
The Kargil conflict took place in:|1999|1965|1971|2008|It was fought in the Kargil sector of Jammu and Kashmir.
The Pakistan National Alliance (PNA) launched a movement in 1977 against the government of:|Zulfikar Ali Bhutto|Ayub Khan|Yahya Khan|Zia-ul-Haq|The PNA alleged rigging in the March 1977 elections.
Z.A. Bhutto was hanged on:|4 April 1979|5 July 1977|16 December 1971|12 October 1999|He was convicted in a murder case; his book 'If I Am Assassinated' was written in jail.
The Eighth Amendment (1985) is known for giving the President power to:|Dissolve the National Assembly (Article 58(2)(b))|Abolish Senate|Create provinces|Ban parties|It was removed by the 13th Amendment (1997), restored by the 17th (2003) and finally removed by the 18th (2010).
The Legal Framework Order (LFO) was issued in:|2002|1977|1985|1999|It was issued by Musharraf before the October 2002 elections and was later validated by the 17th Amendment.
The 17th Amendment (2003) restored the President's power to:|Dissolve the National Assembly|Impose martial law|Pass money bills|Appoint Chief Justice|It was passed under the Musharraf government and removed in 2010.
The 18th Amendment was passed in:|April 2010|1985|1997|2003|It was passed under PM Yousaf Raza Gilani and President Asif Ali Zardari. It renamed NWFP to Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and abolished the Concurrent List.
Baghdad Pact, which Pakistan joined, was signed in:|1955|1948|1965|1971|It was later renamed CENTO. Pakistan joined SEATO in 1954.
Pakistan became a member of SEATO in:|1954|1947|1971|1990|SEATO (South-East Asia Treaty Organization) was created in Manila in 1954.
Pakistan left the Commonwealth in 1972 and rejoined in:|1989|1980|2001|1999|Its membership was suspended in 1999 and 2007 due to military takeovers.
Pakistan became a republic on:|23 March 1956|14 August 1947|11 September 1948|10 April 1973|The Dominion status ended with the enforcement of the 1956 Constitution.
Islamabad was selected as the capital in:|1959-60 (construction began 1961)|1947|1973|1985|Karachi was first capital; Rawalpindi served as interim capital. The Greek planner C.A. Doxiadis designed Islamabad.
The Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission was set up in:|1956|1947|1974|1998|The first nuclear power plant, KANUPP in Karachi, started in 1972.
Pakistan's first satellite Badr-1 was launched in:|1990|1960|2000|2011|It was launched from China. PakSat-1R and later satellites followed.
Dr Abdus Salam won the Nobel Prize in Physics in:|1979|1968|1990|2014|He shared the prize for electroweak unification and was the first Pakistani Nobel laureate.
The first Muslim woman Prime Minister in the world was:|Benazir Bhutto|Tansu Ciller|Sheikh Hasina|Khaleda Zia|She became PM of Pakistan in December 1988 at the age of 35.
Benazir Bhutto was assassinated on:|27 December 2007|27 January 2008|2 May 2011|10 April 1979|She was killed after a rally at Liaquat Bagh, Rawalpindi.
The Lawyers' Movement began in March 2007 after the suspension of:|Chief Justice Iftikhar Muhammad Chaudhry|Chief Justice Saeed-uz-Zaman Siddiqui|Chief Justice Anwar-ul-Haq|Chief Justice Sajjad Ali Shah|The movement led to his restoration in 2009.
The word 'Pakistan' was coined by:|Chaudhry Rahmat Ali|Allama Iqbal|Jinnah|Sir Syed|It appeared in the pamphlet 'Now or Never' (1933), published at Cambridge.
The Cripps Mission came to India in:|1942|1946|1935|1930|It offered Dominion status after WWII but was rejected by both Congress and League.
The Simla Conference was held in:|1945|1946|1942|1947|Viceroy Wavell convened it; it failed because of the dispute over Muslim representation.
The Cabinet Mission arrived in India in:|1946|1942|1940|1947|Its Plan envisaged a loose federation but was not implemented.
Direct Action Day was observed by the Muslim League on:|16 August 1946|14 August 1947|23 March 1940|3 June 1947|It was followed by riots in Calcutta.
The last Viceroy of British India was:|Lord Mountbatten|Lord Wavell|Lord Linlithgow|Lord Irwin|Mountbatten announced the Partition plan on 3 June 1947.
The Indian Independence Act was passed by the British Parliament in:|July 1947|June 1947|August 1947|January 1948|It received Royal Assent on 18 July 1947.
Liaquat Ali Khan presented the so-called 'Poor Man's Budget' in 1947 as:|Finance Minister in the Interim Government|Prime Minister of Pakistan|Governor-General|Viceroy|It taxed the wealthy industrialists, mostly Hindus, and angered Congress.
The first Constituent Assembly of Pakistan met at Karachi on:|10 August 1947|14 August 1947|11 September 1948|23 March 1956|Jinnah was elected its President. The famous speech of 11 August 1947 was delivered there.
The first Constitution of Pakistan was drafted by the:|Second Constituent Assembly under Chaudhry Muhammad Ali|Jinnah|Ayub Khan|Zia-ul-Haq|The 1956 Constitution gave parity between East and West Pakistan and introduced One Unit.
The One Unit scheme of West Pakistan was announced in:|1955|1947|1970|1962|It merged the provinces of West Pakistan into one unit, which was dissolved by Yahya Khan in 1970.
The Government of India Act 1935 introduced which feature in the provinces?|Provincial autonomy|Direct rule|Abolition of legislatures|Universal adult franchise|It set up provincial autonomy and the 1937 elections.
