Find the next number in the series: 2, 4, 8, 16, ___|32|24|30|20|Each term is double the previous: 2 × 2 = 4, 4 × 2 = 8, 8 × 2 = 16, so next is 32. This is a geometric series with ratio 2.
Find the next number: 3, 6, 9, 12, ___|15|14|16|18|The series is multiples of 3 (an arithmetic series with common difference 3).
Find the next number: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, ___|36|30|35|49|These are perfect squares: 1², 2², 3², 4², 5², so next is 6² = 36.
Find the next number: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, ___|13|12|14|15|These are prime numbers in order. After 11 comes 13.
Find the next number: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ___|13|11|12|14|This is the Fibonacci sequence where each term is the sum of the previous two: 5 + 8 = 13.
Find the missing term: 5, 10, 20, 40, ___|80|60|70|50|The ratio is 2, so 40 × 2 = 80.
Find the next number: 100, 90, 80, 70, ___|60|65|50|55|It is a decreasing arithmetic series with difference −10.
