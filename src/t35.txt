# Test 35 - English II: antonyms, one-word substitutions, idioms and phrasal verbs
Choose the word opposite in meaning to ABUNDANT:|Scarce|Plentiful|Ample|Copious|Abundant means plentiful, so its antonym is scarce.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to BRAVE:|Cowardly|Bold|Fearless|Daring|A coward lacks courage.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to VICTORY:|Defeat|Triumph|Success|Win|Victory is success in a contest.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to TEMPORARY:|Permanent|Brief|Short|Fleeting|Permanent means lasting for ever.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to ACCEPT:|Reject|Take|Receive|Agree|Refuse is a synonym of reject.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to ENEMY:|Friend|Foe|Rival|Opponent|Foe, rival and opponent are synonyms of enemy.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to INNOCENT:|Guilty|Pure|Naive|Blameless|Guilty means responsible for an offence.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to ASCEND:|Descend|Rise|Climb|Mount|Descend means to go down.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to FRAGILE:|Sturdy|Delicate|Brittle|Weak|Sturdy means strongly built.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to PROSPERITY:|Poverty|Wealth|Success|Fortune|Prosperity is the state of being successful and rich.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to PRAISE:|Criticise|Applaud|Commend|Admire|Criticise means to find fault.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to VOLUNTARY:|Compulsory|Willing|Optional|Free|Compulsory means required by rule or law.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to MAJORITY:|Minority|Most|Bulk|Greater|Minority means the smaller number.
One word for 'a person who does not believe in God':|Atheist|Agnostic|Theist|Pagan|An agnostic holds that God's existence is unknowable.
One word for 'a person who can speak two languages':|Bilingual|Linguist|Polyglot|Multilingual|A polyglot knows many languages.
One word for 'a person who is present everywhere':|Omnipresent|Omniscient|Omnipotent|Ubiquitously only|Omniscient = all-knowing; omnipotent = all-powerful.
One word for 'a government by the people':|Democracy|Autocracy|Aristocracy|Monarchy|Autocracy is rule by one person.
One word for 'rule by a few':|Oligarchy|Monarchy|Democracy|Anarchy|Anarchy means absence of government.
One word for 'a place where birds are kept':|Aviary|Apiary|Aquarium|Zoo|An apiary is a place where bees are kept.
One word for 'a place where bees are kept':|Apiary|Aviary|Hatchery|Nursery|Apiculture is beekeeping.
One word for 'the study of ancient things':|Archaeology|Anthropology|Astrology|Geology|Anthropology studies human beings.
One word for 'a book written by a person about his own life':|Autobiography|Biography|Memoir only|Diary|A biography is written by someone else.
One word for 'a person who loves books':|Bibliophile|Philanthropist|Misanthrope|Philatelist|A philatelist collects stamps.
One word for 'a person who hates mankind':|Misanthrope|Philanthropist|Misogynist|Pessimist|A misogynist hates women.
One word for 'a person who loves mankind':|Philanthropist|Misanthrope|Optimist|Altruist|Philanthropy is the love of humanity shown through charity.
One word for 'killing of a king':|Regicide|Homicide|Suicide|Fratricide|Genocide is killing of a race; infanticide is killing of an infant.
One word for 'the killing of a baby':|Infanticide|Genocide|Homicide|Patricide|Patricide = killing a father.
One word for 'a speech delivered without preparation':|Extempore|Soliloquy|Monologue|Oration|A soliloquy is talking to oneself.
One word for 'a talk by one person':|Monologue|Dialogue|Soliloquy|Prologue|A dialogue is between two.
One word for 'something that cannot be read':|Illegible|Eligible|Audible|Legible|Eligible means qualified.
One word for 'something that cannot be heard':|Inaudible|Audible|Illegible|Invisible|Invisible cannot be seen.
One word for 'a person who is new to a field':|Novice|Expert|Veteran|Master|Veteran means experienced.
To 'beat about the bush' means:|To avoid coming to the point|To hunt|To fight|To walk fast|The idiom refers to approaching a topic indirectly.
To 'bite the bullet' means:|To face a difficult situation bravely|To eat|To shoot|To argue|It comes from soldiers biting a bullet during surgery.
To 'break the ice' means:|To start a conversation|To melt|To break something|To get angry|It eases social tension.
'A blessing in disguise' means:|A good thing that seems bad at first|A curse|A hidden danger|A holy man|The expression refers to unexpected good fortune.
'Once in a blue moon' means:|Very rarely|Every night|Often|Never|A blue moon is a rare event.
'To call it a day' means:|To stop working|To start work|To make a call|To celebrate|Used when finishing an activity.
'Cost an arm and a leg' means:|Very expensive|Very cheap|Painful|Free|It describes a high price.
'To spill the beans' means:|To reveal a secret|To cook|To waste food|To lose money|It means to disclose confidential information.
'Piece of cake' means:|Something very easy|A sweet|A small portion|A difficult job|It describes a very simple task.
To 'hit the nail on the head' means:|To say exactly the right thing|To do carpentry|To injure oneself|To fail|It means to be accurate about something.
'Under the weather' means:|Slightly ill|Outside in the rain|Very happy|Angry|The phrase is used for feeling unwell.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to ARTIFICIAL:|Natural|Synthetic|Fake|Man-made|Natural means existing in nature.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to DEPART:|Arrive|Leave|Go|Exit|Arrive means to reach a place.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to ANCESTOR:|Descendant|Forefather|Predecessor|Elder|A descendant is a person descended from an ancestor.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to EXTERNAL:|Internal|Outer|Outside|Exterior|Internal = inside.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to NUMEROUS:|Few|Many|Several|Countless|Few = a small number.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to CONFIDENT:|Doubtful|Assured|Certain|Bold|Doubtful = uncertain.
Choose the word opposite in meaning to ZENITH:|Nadir|Peak|Summit|Top|Nadir is the lowest point.
