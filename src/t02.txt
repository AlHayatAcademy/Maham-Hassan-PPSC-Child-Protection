# Test 2 - Punjab Child Protection Law II (practice and procedure)
The primary role of a Child Protection Officer (CPO) is to:|Protect and rehabilitate children at risk through rescue, assessment and referral|Collect taxes|Prosecute offenders in trial courts|Issue national identity cards|A CPO identifies, rescues, assesses and refers vulnerable children, produces them before the Child Protection Court, and follows up on rehabilitation and reintegration.
After taking a child into protective custody, a CPO should first:|Ensure immediate safety, shelter and basic needs|Close the case|Hand the child to any stranger|Post the child's photo online|The immediate priorities are safety, medical needs, food, shelter and emotional comfort before detailed assessment and legal proceedings.
Which approach is recommended when interviewing a child victim?|Child-friendly, non-leading, in a safe setting|Repeated aggressive questioning|Public interview|Leading questions to get a quick confession|Interviews should be conducted by trained persons, using open-ended questions, minimizing repetition, and respecting the child's pace and privacy.
Asking 'He hurt you, didn't he?' is an example of a:|Leading question|Open-ended question|Neutral prompt|Reflective statement|Leading questions suggest the answer and can contaminate a child's testimony. Use open prompts such as 'Tell me what happened'.
The principle of 'do no harm' in child protection means:|Interventions must not expose the child to further risk|Never intervene|Always remove the child from family|Share every detail with all agencies|Even well-meant actions such as rescue, publicity or interviewing can harm a child. Risks must be weighed against benefits in every decision.
Which information about a child victim must be kept confidential?|Name, address, photograph and identifying details|Only the child's age|Nothing|The weather|Confidentiality protects the child from stigma and retaliation. Media are also advised not to reveal identity of victims of abuse.
A social inquiry/assessment report on a child is prepared to:|Assist the court and agency in deciding the best placement and plan|Punish the child|Increase the child's fine|Advertise the Bureau|The report covers family background, circumstances, risks, needs and recommended care plan and is a key document for the Child Protection Court.
A written plan describing a child's needs, goals, services and responsible persons is called:|Individual care plan|Charge sheet|Remand order|Challan|An individual care plan (case plan) is built with the child and family. It sets objectives, timelines and review dates.
Reintegration of a child into his or her family and community after protection services is called:|Social reintegration|Remand|Parole|Retrial|Reintegration should be gradual, supported by follow-up visits, counselling and economic support to the family where needed.
Which type of care is most consistent with international standards for a young child with no parents?|Kinship or foster family care|Large orphanage|Detention centre|Street shelter|Family-based care supports attachment and development. Large institutions are discouraged, especially for children under 3.
A CPO finds a 7-year-old working in a brick kiln. What is the best immediate action?|Remove child from hazardous work, ensure safety and refer to education/protection services|Ignore because of poverty|Pay child more|Wait for the child's 14th birthday|Brick kiln work is a hazardous worst form of child labour. The child must be withdrawn and services provided, while addressing family poverty.
Which of the following is among the 'worst forms of child labour' under ILO Convention 182?|Child trafficking and forced labour|Helping parents at home after school|Light household chores|Participating in school sports|ILO C182 (1999) covers slavery, trafficking, debt bondage, forced recruitment into armed conflict, prostitution/pornography, illicit activities and hazardous work.
ILO Convention 138 (1973) deals with:|Minimum age for admission to employment|Worst forms of child labour|Maternity protection|Forced labour|C138 sets general minimum age at 15 (14 for developing countries), 13–15 for light work and 18 for hazardous work. C182 deals with worst forms.
Pakistan ratified ILO Convention 182 on worst forms of child labour in:|2001|1947|1971|2015|Pakistan ratified C182 in 2001 and C138 in 2006. Pakistan also ratified UNCRC in 1990.
Which is a sign of possible sexual abuse in a young child?|Sudden fear of a particular person and age-inappropriate sexual knowledge|Good appetite|Regular sleep|Active play|Indicators include behavioural changes, regression, nightmares, fear, secrecy and sexualized behaviour. No single sign proves abuse; look at patterns.
When a child discloses abuse to you, the best response is:|Listen calmly, believe, reassure and report|Express shock and disbelief|Promise to keep it secret|Interrogate for every detail|Never promise absolute secrecy. Tell the child you will take help from people who can protect him or her.
A common reason children delay disclosing abuse is:|Fear, shame and threats from the abuser|Love of reporting|Lack of memory always|Being too happy|Many abusers are known to the child and use threats or gifts. Hence delayed or partial disclosure is common.
The majority of child sexual abuse offenders are:|Known to the child|Strangers|Foreign tourists only|Police only|Research globally shows that most offenders are family members or trusted persons. 'Stranger danger' alone is therefore an inadequate safety message.
'Good touch, bad touch' education aims at:|Body safety and teaching children to say no and tell a trusted adult|Making children afraid of all adults|Punishing children|Entertainment only|Body safety programmes teach private parts, consent, secrets versus surprises and identification of trusted adults.
Which of the following is a statutory duty of a school teacher towards child protection?|Report suspected abuse to the appropriate authority|Punish the child|Hide the incident|Move the child to another class without telling anyone|Teachers see children daily and are well placed to notice signs of abuse; schools should have referral pathways and a focal person.
Corporal punishment in schools is:|Harmful and prohibited as per child rights standards and directives|Recommended discipline|Part of the syllabus|Required by law|UNCRC (Art. 19, 28) and the Committee's General Comment 8 condemn corporal punishment. Federal law (ICT) prohibits it and Punjab education department directives ban it.
The Rescue 1122 is an emergency service mainly for:|Medical, fire and rescue emergencies|Child custody cases only|Tax collection|Election matters|1122 provides emergency ambulance, fire and rescue. A child in medical danger should be referred to it while 1121 handles child protection matters.
An abandoned newborn found in a street should be:|Taken to safety, given medical care and reported to the authorities for registration and placement|Left there|Given to the first person who asks|Sold to a family|Abandoned infants are children in need of care and protection. A CPO ensures health care, police report, birth registration and legal placement.
Which of the following best describes a 'safe house' or 'shelter' for children?|Temporary protective residence providing safety and services|A prison|A hospital only|A coaching centre|Shelters provide short-term accommodation, food, counselling and links to long-term solutions. They must be child-friendly and well supervised.
The term 'diversion' in juvenile justice means:|Handling a child offender outside formal court proceedings|Sending him to jail|Increasing the sentence|Hiding the crime|Diversion uses cautions, mediation or community programmes to avoid the harmful effects of formal prosecution. It is encouraged under UNCRC Art. 40.
Who should be present when a child in conflict with the law is questioned?|Parent/guardian or legal aid and social worker|Nobody|Media|Only the complainant|A child has the right to legal assistance and the presence of a parent or guardian during proceedings, in a child-friendly environment.
Detention of a child should be:|A measure of last resort and for the shortest appropriate period|The first option|Permanent|Mandatory for all offences|UNCRC Article 37(b) states that arrest, detention or imprisonment of a child must be used only as a last resort. Children must be separated from adults.
Which of the following is a function of the Child Protection Court?|Decide custody and care of destitute or neglected children|Try murder cases only|Settle land revenue disputes|Conduct elections|It issues orders for placement, reunification, foster care or institutional care and reviews them periodically.
Who must a CPO collaborate with for a complete child protection response?|Police, health, education, social welfare, NGOs and the community|Only banks|Only media|Only foreign embassies|A multi-agency approach is vital. No single agency has all the resources and legal powers for protection.
A multidisciplinary team in child protection typically includes:|Social worker, psychologist, doctor, lawyer and police|Only cooks|Only drivers|Only accountants|Teams share information, make joint assessments and ensure a holistic response.
The term 'psychosocial support' refers to:|Support addressing psychological and social wellbeing|Only medicines|Only legal help|Only money|It includes counselling, play therapy, peer support and recreational activities that help the child recover from trauma and rebuild relationships.
The 'Strengths-based approach' in social work focuses on:|Resources and capacities of the child and family|Only deficits|Punishment|Blaming parents|It builds on existing resilience, skills and supports rather than only problems. It improves engagement and outcomes.
A 'safety plan' for a child at risk is:|An agreed set of actions to keep the child safe from immediate harm|A building map|A school timetable|A court fine|It lists warning signs, safe people, places and steps the child and family will follow, and who to call in an emergency.
Which is the main purpose of follow-up visits after reintegration?|Monitor wellbeing and prevent relapse into risk|Check the neighbours|Find new children for photographs|Collect donations|Follow-up ensures the placement is safe and stable, services are accessible and the child is attending school.
In child protection documentation, case records should be:|Accurate, factual, dated and securely stored|Casual and speculative|Shared on social media|Destroyed immediately|Good records support continuity, accountability and legal processes. Opinion must be separated from fact.
Which action violates a child's privacy?|Posting a rescued child's face on Facebook|Using initials in reports|Securing files in a locked cabinet|Obtaining informed consent|Publishing identity can re-traumatize and endanger the child and violates confidentiality and Article 16 of the UNCRC.
The principle of 'informed consent/assent' with children means:|Explaining in simple language and respecting the child's views|Deciding without asking|Forcing signatures|Ignoring age|Children are consulted in line with Article 12 and their age and maturity. Parental consent is also sought where appropriate and safe.
The Child Protection Information Management System (CPIMS) is used to:|Record and track child protection cases|Print currency|Plan roads|Issue licences|Case management data systems provide referrals, follow-up and trend analysis. Data must be protected under strict confidentiality rules.
'Mandatory reporting' means:|Certain professionals are legally required to report suspected abuse|Reporting is forbidden|Only parents report|Reporting is optional always|Many countries oblige teachers, doctors and social workers to report reasonable suspicion of abuse. It helps early detection.
Which of the following is a primary function of a Child Protection Unit (CPU) at district level?|Receive complaints, rescue children and coordinate services|Run a tax office|Issue passports|Manage railways|CPUs act as district-level focal points for reporting, rapid response and referral. They connect communities, police and the Bureau.
For children with disabilities, which is the best practice?|Inclusive services and accessible protection|Hide them at home|Institutionalize all|Exclude from school|Children with disabilities face higher risk of abuse. The UN CRPD (2006) and Art. 23 of UNCRC require inclusion and special care.
Pakistan ratified the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities in:|2011|1990|1973|2020|Pakistan ratified CRPD in July 2011. Protecting children with disabilities is a part of inclusive child protection.
The most appropriate response to a runaway child is:|Understand reasons, ensure safety and offer family mediation or alternative care|Beat the child|Return immediately without inquiry|Detain in a police lockup|Children run away because of abuse, conflict or poverty. Returning to an unsafe home can cause harm, so assessment is vital.
Which of the following describes 'child participation'?|Children expressing views and influencing decisions affecting them|Children running the organization alone|Children signing contracts|Entertainment programmes only|Article 12 of the UNCRC gives the right to express views freely. Participation should be safe, voluntary and meaningful, not tokenistic.
The ecological model of child protection (Bronfenbrenner) examines risks at:|Individual, family, community and society levels|Only the child|Only government|Only school|Interventions work best when they address several levels at once, e.g., a parenting programme, community norms and policy.
What is the aim of the 'parenting skills' programmes?|Prevent abuse and promote positive discipline|Punish parents|Create more institutions|Raise taxes|Positive parenting uses praise, boundaries and communication instead of harsh punishment. It reduces violence in the home.
Which one is an example of 'positive discipline'?|Setting clear rules and using time-out and praise|Beating|Screaming|Threatening abandonment|Positive discipline teaches self-control and respect. It does not use physical or humiliating punishment.
Which of the following best defines 'rehabilitation' of a child victim?|Restoring physical, psychological and social wellbeing|Only punishing the abuser|Closing the file|Paying fines|Rehabilitation includes health care, counselling, education, skills training and family or community support.
'Vocational training' for older children under protection aims at:|Equipping them with livelihood skills|Making them work in unsafe jobs|Keeping them idle|Recreation only|Skills and education help older adolescents move to independence and avoid re-victimization.
Which agency is mainly responsible for registering births in Pakistan?|NADRA together with Union Councils|Customs|Excise department|WAPDA|Birth registration is done at Union Council/Local Government offices and NADRA issues the B-Form. Registration is the first step to access rights and services.
