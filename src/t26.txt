# Test 26 - World Geography II: rivers, lakes, seas, straits, mountains, deserts and nicknames
The Amazon River flows mainly through:|Brazil|Argentina|India|Egypt|It discharges the largest volume of water of any river.
The Nile flows into the:|Mediterranean Sea|Red Sea|Black Sea|Persian Gulf|It flows northward through Egypt.
The Yangtze is the longest river of:|China|India|Japan|Russia|It flows into the East China Sea at Shanghai.
The Ganges flows into the:|Bay of Bengal|Arabian Sea|Persian Gulf|Red Sea|It is sacred to Hindus and passes through India and Bangladesh.
The Tigris and Euphrates flow through:|Iraq|Iran|Egypt|Turkey only|Mesopotamia means 'land between the rivers'.
The Danube flows into the:|Black Sea|Baltic Sea|North Sea|Mediterranean Sea|It passes through ten countries, including Austria and Hungary.
The Rhine flows mostly through:|Germany|Spain|Italy|Poland|It ends in the North Sea in the Netherlands.
The Volga is the longest river in:|Europe|Asia|Africa|North America|It flows into the Caspian Sea.
The Thames flows through:|London|Paris|Rome|Berlin|The Seine flows through Paris; the Tiber through Rome; the Spree through Berlin.
The Mississippi flows in:|USA|Canada|Mexico|Brazil|It empties into the Gulf of Mexico.
The deepest lake in the world is:|Lake Baikal|Lake Tanganyika|Lake Superior|Lake Victoria|Baikal in Russia is over 1,600 m deep and holds the most fresh water.
The largest freshwater lake by area is:|Lake Superior|Lake Victoria|Lake Baikal|Lake Michigan|It is one of the Great Lakes of North America.
The Dead Sea lies between:|Jordan and Israel/Palestine|Egypt and Sudan|Iran and Iraq|Turkey and Greece|Its shore is the lowest land point on Earth.
The Suez Canal connects:|Mediterranean and Red Sea|Atlantic and Pacific|Black and Caspian Sea|Arabian Sea and Persian Gulf|It opened in 1869 and shortens the Europe-Asia route.
The Panama Canal connects:|Atlantic and Pacific Oceans|Indian and Pacific Oceans|Red Sea and Mediterranean|Baltic and North Sea|It opened in 1914.
The Strait of Hormuz connects:|Persian Gulf and the Gulf of Oman|Red Sea and Mediterranean|Black Sea and Aegean|Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal|About a fifth of the world's oil passes through it.
The Strait of Gibraltar separates:|Spain and Morocco|Italy and Greece|Turkey and Cyprus|India and Sri Lanka|It connects the Atlantic and the Mediterranean.
The Bosphorus Strait is in:|Turkey|Greece|Egypt|Iran|It divides Istanbul into European and Asian sides.
The Bab-el-Mandeb strait connects:|Red Sea and the Gulf of Aden|Persian Gulf and Arabian Sea|Mediterranean and Atlantic|Black Sea and Caspian Sea|It lies between Yemen and Djibouti/Eritrea.
The Malacca Strait lies between:|Malay Peninsula and Sumatra|India and Sri Lanka|Japan and Korea|Australia and New Zealand|It is one of the busiest shipping lanes.
The English Channel separates England from:|France|Spain|Ireland|Norway|The Channel Tunnel links the UK with France.
The Palk Strait separates India from:|Sri Lanka|Maldives|Pakistan|Myanmar|It connects the Bay of Bengal and the Palk Bay.
The Himalayan range is located mainly in:|Asia|Europe|South America|Africa|It includes Everest, Kangchenjunga and Nanga Parbat.
The Andes mountain range is in:|South America|Africa|Asia|Europe|It is the longest continental mountain range.
The Alps are located mainly in:|Europe|Asia|Africa|North America|Mont Blanc is the highest peak.
The Rockies are in:|North America|South America|Europe|Asia|They stretch from Canada to the USA.
The Atlas mountains are in:|North Africa|South Africa|Asia|Europe|They stretch across Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia.
Mount Kilimanjaro, the highest peak of Africa, is in:|Tanzania|Kenya|Uganda|Ethiopia|It is a dormant volcano, 5,895 m high.
The highest mountain of North America is:|Denali (Mount McKinley)|Mount Logan|Mount Whitney|Mount Rainier|Denali in Alaska is about 6,190 m.
The highest mountain of Europe is:|Mount Elbrus|Mont Blanc|Matterhorn|Mount Olympus|Mount Elbrus in Russia is about 5,642 m; Mont Blanc is the highest in the Alps.
The highest mountain of South America is:|Aconcagua|Chimborazo|Huascaran|Cotopaxi|Aconcagua in Argentina is about 6,961 m.
The highest mountain of Antarctica is:|Vinson Massif|Mount Erebus|Mount Kosciuszko|Mount Cook|Mount Kosciuszko is the highest in Australia.
The Gobi desert lies in:|China and Mongolia|India|Saudi Arabia|Africa|It is a cold desert.
The Atacama desert, one of the driest places on Earth, is in:|Chile|Argentina|Peru|Brazil|Some weather stations there have never recorded rainfall.
The Rub' al Khali (Empty Quarter) is in:|Arabian Peninsula|Sahara|Central Asia|Australia|It is one of the largest sand deserts.
The Kalahari desert is mainly in:|Botswana|Egypt|Libya|Mali|It also extends into Namibia and South Africa.
The 'Land of the Rising Sun' is:|Japan|China|India|Korea|The Japanese name Nippon means 'origin of the sun'.
The 'Land of the Midnight Sun' is generally:|Norway|Sweden only|Japan|Canada|Norway (Arctic regions) experiences midnight sun in summer.
The 'Land of White Elephant' is:|Thailand|Burma|India|Sri Lanka|Thailand's old flag featured a white elephant.
The 'Land of Thousand Lakes' is:|Finland|Sweden|Norway|Iceland|Finland has about 188,000 lakes.
The 'Island Continent' is:|Australia|Greenland|Madagascar|Borneo|It is both a country and a continent.
The 'Eternal City' is:|Rome|Athens|Paris|Jerusalem|Rome is nicknamed Caput Mundi.
The 'City of Canals' is:|Venice|Amsterdam|Bangkok|Stockholm|Venice has about 118 islands and 400 bridges.
The 'Big Apple' is:|New York City|Chicago|Los Angeles|Washington|The nickname dates from the 1920s.
The 'Windy City' is:|Chicago|Boston|Seattle|Denver|Chicago is in Illinois on Lake Michigan.
The 'Sick Man of Europe' referred to:|The Ottoman Empire|Greece|Spain|Italy|The phrase was used in the nineteenth century.
The International Date Line runs roughly along the longitude:|180 degrees|0 degrees|90 degrees East|90 degrees West|The Prime Meridian is at 0 degrees and passes through Greenwich.
The Prime Meridian passes through:|Greenwich, London|Paris|Cairo|Tokyo|Longitude 0 degrees divides the Earth into Eastern and Western Hemispheres.
