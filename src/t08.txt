# Test 1 - Punjab Child Protection Law I
The Child Protection and Welfare Bureau in Punjab works under which law?|Punjab Destitute and Neglected Children Act 2004|Juvenile Justice System Ordinance 2000|Employment of Children Act 1991|Guardians and Wards Act 1890|The Punjab Destitute and Neglected Children Act (PDNCA) 2004 is the parent law for care, protection, maintenance, welfare, education and rehabilitation of destitute and neglected children in Punjab. The Bureau and its Child Protection Officers act under it.
Under the Punjab Destitute and Neglected Children Act 2004 a 'child' is a person below the age of:|18 years|16 years|14 years|21 years|The Act follows the UNCRC (Article 1) definition: every human being below 18 is a child. Do not confuse with labour laws where 14 or 15 is used for employment, or the Child Marriage Restraint Act where 16 applies to girls.
The toll-free helpline of the Child Protection and Welfare Bureau Punjab is:|1121|1122|15|1099|1121 is the Child Protection Bureau helpline. 1122 is Rescue 1122 (emergency), 15 is Police, and 1099 is the Ministry of Human Rights helpline (also used for Zainab Alert reporting).
Cases of destitute and neglected children in Punjab are produced before the:|Child Protection Court|Anti-Terrorism Court|Banking Court|Customs Court|The PDNCA 2004 provides for Child Protection Courts, which decide on custody, care and placement of children found destitute or neglected. A Child Protection Officer produces the child before the Court within the legally prescribed time.
Which of the following is NOT a form of child abuse?|Providing age-appropriate immunization|Hitting a child with a stick|Constantly humiliating a child|Forcing a child into sexual activity|Abuse is classified as physical, emotional/psychological, sexual abuse and neglect. Immunization is a protective health service. Beating, humiliation and sexual exploitation are abuse.
Failure of a caregiver to provide food, shelter, medical care, education or supervision is called:|Neglect|Corporal punishment|Trafficking|Delinquency|Neglect is an act of omission (failing to act) whereas abuse is typically an act of commission. Neglect can be physical, medical, educational or emotional and is the most common form of child maltreatment worldwide.
The guiding principle that a child's welfare must be the primary consideration in every decision is called:|Best interests of the child|Zero tolerance|Parens patriae only|Doctrine of necessity|Best interests of the child is Article 3 of the UNCRC and the core test in case management, court orders and placement decisions. It is one of the four general principles of the Convention.
Which is the correct first step in the child protection case management process?|Identification and registration of the case|Case closure|Follow-up review|Foster placement|The usual sequence is identification and registration, assessment, case planning, implementation and referral, follow-up and review, and finally case closure. Skipping assessment leads to wrong placement decisions.
In Pakistan the identity document for a child below 18 issued by NADRA is called:|Child Registration Certificate (B-Form)|CNIC|NICOP|Smart card for overseas|The Child Registration Certificate, commonly called B-Form (formerly 'Form-B'), is issued to children under 18 and is required for school admission, passports and many child services. Birth registration is a UNCRC Article 7 right.
Article 25A of the Constitution of Pakistan guarantees free and compulsory education to children aged:|5 to 16 years|4 to 14 years|6 to 18 years|5 to 12 years|Article 25A was inserted by the 18th Amendment (2010). The State shall provide free and compulsory education to all children of the age of five to sixteen years.
Which Article of the Constitution of Pakistan prohibits employment of children below 14 years in factories, mines or other hazardous employment?|Article 11(3)|Article 9|Article 14|Article 19|Article 11 prohibits slavery, forced labour and child labour. Clause (3) states that no child below the age of fourteen years shall be engaged in any factory or mine or any other hazardous employment.
The Employment of Children Act, a federal law prohibiting children in certain hazardous occupations, was passed in:|1991|1973|2000|2010|The Employment of Children Act 1991 prohibits employment of children under 14 in listed hazardous occupations and regulates working conditions of adolescents. Provinces have since enacted their own laws after devolution.
The Zainab Alert, Response and Recovery Act was passed in:|2020|2010|2016|2004|The Act was named after seven-year-old Zainab Ansari of Kasur, raped and murdered in January 2018. It created the Zainab Alert Response and Recovery Agency for missing and abducted children under 18.
Zainab Ansari, whose case led to a new law on missing children, belonged to which city?|Kasur|Faisalabad|Multan|Sialkot|The January 2018 incident in Kasur shocked the nation and led to the Zainab Alert Act 2020. Kasur also witnessed the 2015 Husain Khanwala child abuse video scandal.
The Guardians and Wards Act 1890 primarily deals with:|Appointment and custody of guardians for minors|Registration of marriages|Employment of children|Juvenile trials|Pakistan has no general adoption law for Muslims; the Guardians and Wards Act 1890 governs guardianship and custody of minors. The welfare of the minor is the paramount consideration for the Guardian Court.
Kafalah in Islamic law is best described as:|Guardianship and care of a child without changing lineage|Full legal adoption with inheritance rights|Child marriage|Compulsory education|Kafalah allows a family to care for and raise a child while the child keeps his or her biological identity and lineage. It is accepted in Islam, unlike adoption that changes parentage.
Placing a child with a family other than his or her own for temporary care is called:|Foster care|Remand|Probation|Parole|Foster care is a temporary family-based alternative care arrangement. Best practice puts family-based care before institutional care and aims at reunification when safe.
Which principle states that institutional care of a child should be used only as the last resort?|Alternative care hierarchy|Doctrine of res judicata|Principle of legality|Ultra vires|Under the UN Guidelines for Alternative Care of Children, family-based care and kinship care are preferred; institutional placement is a temporary, last resort option and should be reviewed regularly.
Tracing the parents or relatives of a separated child and reuniting them is called:|Family tracing and reunification (FTR)|Case closure|Probation|Rehabilitation only|FTR is a core service in child protection, especially for lost, trafficked or street children. It is done only when reunification is in the child's best interest and does not expose the child to harm.
Which of these is an example of emotional abuse?|Repeatedly threatening and ridiculing a child|Reading to a child|Regular health checkups|Praising effort|Emotional or psychological abuse includes rejecting, terrorizing, isolating, ridiculing or constantly belittling a child. It damages self-esteem and can cause long-term mental health problems.
Which of the following correctly describes trafficking of a child under the UN Palermo Protocol?|Recruitment, transport or receipt for exploitation, even without force or coercion|Only forced marriage|Only crossing borders illegally|Only domestic labour|For children (under 18) the 'means' element (force, fraud, coercion) need not be proved; recruitment, transfer, harbouring or receipt for the purpose of exploitation is trafficking.
The Prevention of Trafficking in Persons Act, a federal law of Pakistan, was passed in:|2018|1860|1992|2022|PTPA 2018 and the Prevention of Smuggling of Migrants Act 2018 replaced the Prevention and Control of Human Trafficking Ordinance 2002. Trafficking is exploitation-oriented, smuggling is consent-based illegal border crossing.
Under the Child Marriage Restraint Act 1929 (as applicable in Punjab) the minimum age of marriage for a girl is:|16 years|14 years|18 years|21 years|The 1929 Act sets 16 for girls and 18 for boys. Child rights groups demand 18 for both in line with the UNCRC; Sindh and Islamabad have enacted 18 for both.
Under the Juvenile Justice System Ordinance 2000 a 'juvenile' means a person who has not attained the age of:|18 years|16 years|14 years|21 years|The JJSO 2000 defined a juvenile as under 18 and introduced diversion and child-friendly procedure. It was later replaced at federal level by the Juvenile Justice System Act 2018.
Which of the following is a primary prevention activity in child protection?|Community awareness campaign on safe parenting|Prosecuting an abuser|Rehabilitating an abused child|Placing a child in foster care|Primary prevention targets the whole population before abuse happens. Secondary prevention targets at-risk groups; tertiary prevention works with victims and offenders after abuse has occurred.
Which is a common risk factor for child abuse and neglect?|Poverty, family violence and parental substance abuse|High parental education|Stable housing|Strong social support|Risk factors increase the likelihood of maltreatment; protective factors (social support, parenting skills, stable income) reduce it. Assessment should look at both.
Which principle is most important when handling child helpline calls?|Confidentiality and child's safety|Publishing the child's name|Ignoring anonymous calls|Calling the media|Child helplines protect identity and share information only on a need-to-know basis, except where disclosure is required to protect the child from serious harm.
Children who live and work on the street but return home at night are called:|Children ON the street|Children OF the street|Orphans|Foundlings|Children on the street keep family links; children of the street live on the street with little or no family contact. Interventions differ: family strengthening versus long-term residential or alternative care.
Which law is commonly used against begging by adults and exploitation of children for begging in Punjab?|Punjab Vagrancy Ordinance 1958|Punjab Food Authority Act|West Pakistan Arms Ordinance|Punjab Tenancy Act|The Punjab Vagrancy Ordinance 1958 criminalizes begging and sets up vagrant homes. Using children for begging is also exploitation and a child protection concern under PDNCA.
Under Section 82 of the Pakistan Penal Code (as amended in 2016) nothing is an offence if done by a child under the age of:|10 years|7 years|12 years|14 years|The Criminal Laws (Amendment) Act 2016 raised the age of criminal responsibility from 7 to 10 years. Section 83 now covers a child above 10 and under 14 who has not attained sufficient maturity.
Which PPC section criminalizes sexual abuse of children (inserted in 2016)?|Section 377A|Section 302|Section 420|Section 489F|Sections 377A and 377B deal with sexual abuse and its punishment. Sections 292A–292C deal with pornography involving children (display to a child, child pornography, cruelty).
Child pornography is criminalized under Section ___ of the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016.|22|10|3|37|PECA 2016 Section 22 punishes producing, distributing or possessing sexually explicit images of a minor. Section 21 deals with offences against modesty and dignity of a natural person.
The National Commission on the Rights of Child Act was enacted in:|2017|1973|2009|2021|The NCRC Act 2017 set up a federal Commission to monitor the implementation of child rights and to coordinate with provinces. Its parent ministry is the Ministry of Human Rights.
UNICEF stands for:|United Nations International Children's Emergency Fund (now United Nations Children's Fund)|United Nations Internal Child Education Fund|Universal Network for Children and Families|United Nations Institute for Child Education|UNICEF was created in 1946 to help children after World War II. It kept the acronym but the name changed in 1953 to United Nations Children's Fund. HQ: New York.
Save the Children was founded in 1919 by:|Eglantyne Jebb|Henri Dunant|Florence Nightingale|Hermann Gmeiner|Eglantyne Jebb drafted the 1923 Declaration of the Rights of the Child, adopted by the League of Nations in 1924. Hermann Gmeiner founded SOS Children's Villages (1949).
SOS Children's Villages were founded by:|Hermann Gmeiner|Eglantyne Jebb|Janusz Korczak|Maria Montessori|Hermann Gmeiner opened the first SOS Children's Village in Imst, Austria in 1949. The model uses family-style care by an 'SOS mother'.
The Probation of Offenders Ordinance was promulgated in:|1960|1947|1984|2005|The Probation of Offenders Ordinance 1960 allows courts to release suitable offenders, including juveniles, under supervision of a probation officer rather than imprison them.
The Reformatory Schools Act dates from:|1897|1860|1923|1950|The Reformatory Schools Act 1897 provided for the detention of youthful offenders in reformatory schools for education and training. It is among the oldest juvenile legislation in the subcontinent.
Which of the following is the most important element of a child protection policy of an organization?|Code of conduct and reporting procedures for staff|Fundraising targets|Office decoration|Marketing slogan|A child safeguarding policy sets standards of behaviour, safe recruitment, reporting and response procedures to keep children safe from harm caused by staff, volunteers and partners.

Which one is a protective factor against child maltreatment?|Strong parent-child bond and social support|Domestic violence|Parental drug use|Isolation|Protective factors buffer risk. Programmes thus build parenting skills, resilience, community networks and access to services.

The term 'in loco parentis' means:|In the place of a parent|Against the parent|By law|Before the court|Teachers, orphanage staff and guardians act in loco parentis and hold a duty of care towards the child in their charge.
