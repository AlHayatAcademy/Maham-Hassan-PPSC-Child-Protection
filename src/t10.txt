# Test 6 - Social Work II, counselling, welfare and research
The founder of client-centered (person-centered) counselling is:|Carl Rogers|Sigmund Freud|B.F. Skinner|Albert Ellis|Rogers emphasized unconditional positive regard, empathy and genuineness as core conditions for change.
Rational Emotive Behaviour Therapy (REBT) was developed by:|Albert Ellis|Aaron Beck|Carl Jung|Carl Rogers|Ellis proposed the ABC model: Activating event, Beliefs, Consequences. Irrational beliefs are challenged and replaced.
Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) was developed by:|Aaron T. Beck|Ivan Pavlov|Abraham Maslow|Erik Erikson|Beck's cognitive therapy treats depression by changing negative thought patterns. CBT is evidence-based for trauma, anxiety and depression, including trauma-focused CBT for children.
Egan's SOLER micro-skills for attending stand for:|Squarely, Open posture, Lean, Eye contact, Relax|Speak, Observe, Listen, Evaluate, Respond|Study, Observe, Learn, Explain, Review|Sit, Open, Look, Eat, Rest|SOLER is a helpful acronym for non-verbal attending in counselling interviews.
Motivational Interviewing was developed by:|William Miller and Stephen Rollnick|Beck and Ellis|Freud and Jung|Piaget and Vygotsky|MI is a collaborative, goal-oriented style that strengthens motivation for change, widely used in addiction work.
Reflection in counselling means:|Mirroring the client's feelings and meaning back|Giving advice|Changing the topic|Judging|It conveys understanding and helps the client explore feelings further.
Which is a barrier to effective listening?|Prejudging the client|Eye contact|Paraphrasing|Silence used wisely|Biases, distractions, interrupting and giving premature advice hinder listening.
Confidentiality may be breached when:|There is serious risk of harm to the client or others (e.g., child abuse)|The client is annoyed|A friend asks|The worker is curious|Limits of confidentiality should be explained at the beginning of the helping relationship, especially in child protection.
Informed consent means the client:|Understands the nature, risks and benefits and agrees voluntarily|Signs without reading|Is forced|Is misled|Competent clients should be given information in plain language. For children assent plus parental or legal consent is sought.
A non-governmental organization (NGO) is:|A non-profit voluntary organization independent of the government|A government department|A for-profit company|A political party|NGOs operate for welfare, development or advocacy purposes and are registered under laws such as the Societies Registration Act 1860 or Companies Ordinance Section 42 (now Section 42 of Companies Act 2017).
In Pakistan, voluntary social welfare agencies are registered under:|Voluntary Social Welfare Agencies (Registration and Control) Ordinance 1961|Police Order 2002|Customs Act 1969|Arms Ordinance|VSWA Ordinance 1961 regulates registration and control of voluntary social welfare agencies by the Social Welfare Department.
The 'Edhi Foundation' was founded by:|Abdul Sattar Edhi|Akhtar Hameed Khan|Amjad Saqib|Imran Khan|Edhi started in 1951 in Karachi; his ambulance network is among the largest in the world. He was famously called 'Angel of Mercy'.
Akhuwat, an interest-free microfinance organization, was founded in 2001 by:|Dr. Muhammad Amjad Saqib|Dr. Akhtar Hameed Khan|Dr. Sania Nishtar|Dr. Abdul Qadeer|Akhuwat gives Qarz-e-Hasna (interest-free loans) based on the principle of Islamic brotherhood (mawakhat).
The Orangi Pilot Project (OPP) in Karachi was established by:|Akhtar Hameed Khan|Edhi|Arif Hasan|Amjad Saqib|Dr. Akhtar Hameed Khan started OPP in 1980 for community-managed sanitation. He also pioneered the Comilla Model of rural development.
The Comilla model of cooperative rural development is associated with:|Akhtar Hameed Khan|Mahbub ul Haq|Edhi|Fatima Jinnah|Comilla Academy for Rural Development in East Pakistan (1959) introduced a two-tier cooperative system.
The Aga Khan Foundation was set up in:|1967|1947|1990|2001|AKF is part of the Aga Khan Development Network and works in health, education and rural development.
The Citizens Foundation (TCF) is mainly working in:|Education for underprivileged children|Cricket|Banking|Power|TCF was founded in 1995 and operates schools in urban slums and rural areas.
The Society for the Protection of the Rights of the Child (SPARC) is an organization based in:|Islamabad|London|Delhi|Dhaka|SPARC (1991) works for child rights, monitors child abuse and publishes the 'State of Pakistan's Children' report.
Which NGO is known in Pakistan for working on child sexual abuse prevention?|Sahil|Edhi|Akhuwat|Hum Sab|Sahil collects data and publishes an annual 'Cruel Numbers' report on child sexual abuse cases reported in the media.
Which of the following is the helpline for missing and lost children in Pakistan operated by Roshan/NGO partners?|1138|1121|15|1122|1138 is the Roshni Helpline for missing children, founded by Roshni (a registered NGO in Islamabad). Madadgar Helpline 1098 is also a child helpline.
Zakat is levied at the rate of:|2.5 percent|10 percent|5 percent|20 percent|Zakat is 2.5 percent on savings above nisab held for a year, 10% Ushr on rain-fed crop produce. Zakat funds are used for the needy, orphans and others categories mentioned in Surah At-Tawbah (9:60).
Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal (PBM) mainly provides assistance to:|Destitute, widows, orphans and persons with disabilities|Large industrial houses|Foreign banks|Political parties|PBM is a federal welfare institution funded through public and government resources. It also runs child support and education assistance programmes for needy families.
The Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP) was launched in:|2008|1992|2000|2019|BISP gives unconditional cash transfers to poor women through the National Socioeconomic Registry. Waseela-e-Taleem gives conditional transfers for school attendance.
The Ehsaas Programme was launched in:|2019|2008|2010|2005|Ehsaas, led by Dr. Sania Nishtar, includes Kafaalat, Tahafuz, Ehsaas Education Stipends and Langar schemes.
The Employees' Old-Age Benefits Institution (EOBI) was established in:|1976|1990|2008|1947|EOBI provides old-age pension, invalidity pension and survivor's benefits for workers in private establishments.
The Workers Welfare Fund Ordinance was promulgated in:|1971|1960|1999|2010|WWF provides housing, scholarships and dowry assistance for workers.
Which of the following is a social security scheme for health care of workers?|Employees' Social Security Institution (ESSI)|Pakistan Railways|NAB|Excise|Punjab Employees Social Security Institution gives medical care, maternity and injury benefits.
Needs assessment is:|Systematic process of identifying gaps between current and desired conditions|A court case|A tax return|A random guess|It is a first step in programme planning. It uses surveys, focus groups and secondary data.
A logical framework (log-frame) matrix includes:|Goal, purpose/outcome, outputs, activities, indicators|Only budget|Only staff list|Only photographs|Logframes link inputs to results and describe indicators, means of verification and assumptions.
SMART objectives stand for:|Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound|Simple, Modern, Active, Real, Total|Social, Moral, Artistic, Rich, Trained|Sure, Mature, Adjustable, Ready, True|Good objectives are clear and measurable; time-bound objectives set a deadline.
Monitoring is:|Continuous tracking of activities and progress|Final evaluation only|Closing the project|Fund raising|Monitoring checks that implementation is on track; evaluation assesses relevance, effectiveness, efficiency, impact and sustainability.
SWOT analysis stands for:|Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats|Skills, Work, Order, Time|Study, Write, Organize, Teach|Safety, Welfare, Order, Trust|SWOT is a strategic planning tool. Strengths and weaknesses are internal; opportunities and threats are external.
Participatory Rural Appraisal (PRA) was popularized by:|Robert Chambers|Max Weber|Karl Marx|Piaget|PRA enables local people to analyse their own conditions using tools like mapping, seasonal calendars and transect walks.
The first step of social research is:|Identification of the research problem|Data analysis|Report writing|Publication|A clear problem statement guides objectives, design and methods.
A hypothesis is:|A tentative testable statement about relationship between variables|A proven fact|A theory|A conclusion|A hypothesis is formulated before data collection. The null hypothesis states no relationship.
The variable which is manipulated or considered the cause is called:|Independent variable|Dependent variable|Intervening variable|Constant|The dependent variable is the effect or outcome measured.
Reliability of a research tool means:|Consistency of results over repeated use|Accuracy of what it measures|Cost|Length|Validity means the tool measures what it is supposed to measure. A tool can be reliable without being valid.
Which sampling technique gives every unit an equal chance of selection?|Simple random sampling|Purposive sampling|Convenience sampling|Snowball sampling|Probability sampling (simple random, systematic, stratified, cluster) permits generalization. Non-probability sampling includes quota, purposive, convenience and snowball.
Snowball sampling is useful for:|Hard-to-reach populations like street children|Large populations|Census|Lotteries|Initial participants refer other participants. It is a non-probability method.
A questionnaire is a tool in which:|Respondent writes answers to printed questions|Interviewer records speech|Observer watches|Machine measures|An interview schedule is filled by the interviewer. Questionnaire suits literate respondents.
Qualitative research mainly uses:|Words, narratives and observations|Only numbers|Only graphs|Only equations|Qualitative research explores meanings, e.g., through interviews, focus groups, case studies and ethnography.
A focus group discussion (FGD) usually involves:|6 to 12 people guided by a facilitator|1000 people|1 person|Whole village|FGDs generate views quickly and reveal group dynamics.
The 'mean' is:|Arithmetic average|Middle value|Most frequent value|Difference of extremes|Median is the middle value; mode is the most frequent. Range = highest − lowest.
Informed consent, confidentiality and avoiding harm are principles of:|Research ethics|Taxation|Advertising|Politics|Researchers must also protect vulnerable groups like children. Ethical review is required before studies involving minors.
Social work practice with children and families should be guided by:|Child's best interests and family strengths|Worker's preference only|Donor's preference only|Convenience|Both child safety and family preservation are balanced by careful assessment.
Burnout among social workers is caused by:|Chronic work stress, high caseload and lack of support|Holidays|Good supervision|Adequate pay|Self-care, supervision and peer support are essential in a trauma-exposed job.
Vicarious trauma refers to:|Emotional impact on helpers exposed to others' trauma|Child trauma only|Physical injury|Financial loss|It can include nightmares, numbness and cynicism. Debriefing and supervision reduce its effects.
The term 'social justice' means:|Fair distribution of resources and opportunities in society|Punishment|Equal income|Charity only|It is a core value of social work and has an important role in advocacy for vulnerable groups.
Social welfare differs from social work in that social welfare is:|A broad system of programmes and services; social work is the profession delivering help|The same thing|A tax|A court|Social welfare includes policies; social work is a professional activity within it.
Which is a preventive social welfare service?|Immunization and parenting education|Prison|Court punishment|Fine|Preventive services reduce problems before they occur; curative services treat existing problems; rehabilitative services restore functioning.
