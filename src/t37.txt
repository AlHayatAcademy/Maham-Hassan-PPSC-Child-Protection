Identify the error: 'He do not like tea.'|do not|He|like|tea|It should be 'He does not like tea'.
Identify the error: 'She is more taller than me.'|more taller|She is|than|me|Say 'taller' or 'more tall' but not both.
Identify the error: 'The informations are useful.'|informations|The|are|useful|'Information' is uncountable.
Identify the error: 'I am knowing him since 2010.'|am knowing|I|him|since 2010|Stative verbs like 'know' are not used in continuous tenses.
Identify the error: 'He gave me an advice.'|an advice|He gave|me|No error|'Advice' is uncountable; say 'a piece of advice'.
Identify the error: 'Each of the students have passed.'|have|Each|students|passed|'Each' takes a singular verb: 'has'.
Identify the error: 'One of my friends are a doctor.'|are|One|friends|doctor|The subject 'one' is singular.

Identify the error: 'I prefer reading than writing.'|than|I prefer|reading|writing|'Prefer' takes 'to', not 'than'.
Choose the correctly punctuated sentence:|Is she coming to the party?|Is she coming to the party.|Is she coming to the party!|Is, she coming to the party|A direct question ends with a question mark.
Choose the correct use of the apostrophe:|The children's playground|The childrens' playground|The childrens playground|The children playgrounds'|'Children' is already plural, so add 's.
Choose the correct use of 'its/it's':|The dog wagged its tail.|The dog wagged it's tail.|Its a nice day.|The dog wagged its' tail.|'It's' means 'it is'; 'its' is possessive.
Choose the correct use of 'their/there/they're':|They're waiting for their bus over there.|Their waiting for they're bus over there.|There waiting for there bus over their.|They're waiting for there bus over their.|'They're' = they are; 'their' = possessive; 'there' = place.
Choose the correct use of 'affect/effect':|The rain will affect the crops.|The rain will effect the crops.|The rain will have an affect.|The effect rain the crops.|'Affect' is usually a verb; 'effect' a noun.
Choose the correct use of 'principal/principle':|The principal gave a speech.|The principle gave a speech.|The principle of the school came.|The principals gave speech.|'Principal' = head or main; 'principle' = rule.
Choose the correct use of 'stationary/stationery':|He bought stationery from the shop.|He bought stationary from the shop.|The car was stationery.|Stationary is paper.|'Stationary' means not moving; 'stationery' means writing materials.
Choose the correct use of 'complement/compliment':|She paid me a compliment.|She paid me a complement.|The colours compliment each other.|He complimented the meal's complement.|'Compliment' is praise; 'complement' completes.
Which is a simple sentence?|The child sleeps.|The child sleeps because he is tired.|The child sleeps and the mother reads.|If the child sleeps, we will go.|A simple sentence has one independent clause.
Which sentence is in the future perfect tense?|I will have finished by noon.|I will finish at noon.|I have finished.|I am finishing.|Future perfect = will have + past participle.
Which sentence is in the present perfect continuous tense?|She has been working since morning.|She worked.|She is working.|She will work.|Present perfect continuous = has/have been + -ing.
