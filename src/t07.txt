# Test 10 - Subject Mock I (scenario + mixed subject)
A teacher notices bruises on a student who says his father beats him. The first action should be:|Report the concern to the school's child protection focal person/authority and the helpline|Ignore it as a family matter|Confront the father in public|Post a photo on social media|Teachers must report suspected abuse through the proper channel. Reporting in good faith protects the child and the teacher.
A child tells a CPO she is afraid to go home because of her uncle. The CPO should:|Listen, assess risk and arrange safety before any return|Return her immediately|Tell the uncle|Ask the child to be quiet|Assessment of risk is vital before any decision about returning a child to the home. Do not alert the alleged perpetrator.
A boy of 12 is arrested for theft. Which is correct?|He should be treated under juvenile justice procedures and separated from adults|He should be sent to adult jail|He should be handcuffed|His name should be published|Children in conflict with law are protected by juvenile laws. Detention is a last resort and identity is protected.
A 15-year-old girl is being married by her family. Which approach is best?|Intervene through authorities, counsel family and support the girl|Attend the wedding|Do nothing|Blame the girl|Child marriage violates child rights; interventions combine legal action with community dialogue and support to girls, including education.
A child who is separated from caregivers during a flood should first be:|Registered, kept safe and traced for family reunification|Adopted immediately|Ignored|Sent to work|Emergency child protection includes separated children registration, interim care and tracing.
An 'unaccompanied child' is one who:|Is separated from both parents and is not cared for by an adult responsible for him or her|Is with his father|Is with a teacher|Is in school|Separated children are separated from both parents or previous caregivers but may be with other relatives.
An orphan who has lost both parents is a:|Double orphan|Single orphan|Social orphan|Foundling|UNICEF defines an orphan as a child who lost one or both parents. A foundling is an abandoned child of unknown parentage.
In case of an anonymous tip about child abuse, the proper step is:|Verify and respond as the child may be in danger|Discard it|Publish it|Charge the caller|Anonymous reports should still be assessed on risk and followed up.
Sexual grooming of children online involves:|Building trust with a child to manipulate or exploit|Teaching computers|Playing games alone|Writing code|Online safety education for children and parents and reporting to FIA cyber crime unit are important prevention steps.
Cyberbullying is:|Harassment using digital means|Computer repair|Online education|Online banking|Victims may show anxiety, withdrawal and school refusal. Schools need clear anti-bullying policies.
Which of the following BEST describes 'bullying'?|Repeated intentional aggressive behaviour with a power imbalance|One-time accidental push|Friendly teasing|Competition|Olweus defines bullying as repeated negative actions against a person who has difficulty defending himself.
A child 'in need of special protection' includes:|Street, trafficked, working and disabled children|Only rich children|Only school toppers|Only adults|Special protection measures are for children facing exceptionally difficult circumstances.
The best placement for a lost child whose parents have been traced and are safe is:|Reunification with parents after assessment|Orphanage permanently|Adoption by strangers|Detention|Family reunification is preferred if it is safe.
Which is a proper way to handle media regarding a child abuse case?|Protect identity and give only general information|Release photos|Give name and address|Hold press conference with child|Ethical reporting avoids secondary victimization.
A child is working as a domestic servant at age 11 and is beaten by the employer. Which law/standard is primarily violated?|Child labour prohibitions and protection from abuse|Tax law|Traffic law|Copyright law|Such employment is prohibited by child labour laws; abuse is a criminal offence.
The WHO defines an 'adolescent' as a person aged:|10 to 19 years|5 to 10 years|15 to 30 years|18 to 25 years|The UN defines 'youth' as 15–24 years and the Commonwealth as 15–29 years. Pakistan's National Youth Policy defines youth as 15–29.
The six-month period for which WHO recommends exclusive breastfeeding is:|First 6 months of life|First month|First year only|First 2 years|After 6 months complementary foods are added and breastfeeding continues up to 2 years or beyond.
BCG vaccine protects against:|Tuberculosis|Measles|Polio|Tetanus|BCG is given at birth under the EPI schedule. Pentavalent protects against five diseases (diphtheria, pertussis, tetanus, hepatitis B, Hib).
Which disease remains endemic in Pakistan and Afghanistan?|Polio|Plague|Smallpox|Cholera|Pakistan and Afghanistan are the only two countries with wild poliovirus type 1. Smallpox was eradicated in 1980.
MUAC stands for:|Mid-Upper Arm Circumference|Maternal Under-Age Care|Medical Under Analysis Chart|Measured Uniform Area Code|MUAC below 11.5 cm in a child aged 6–59 months indicates severe acute malnutrition.
Stunting means:|Low height for age due to chronic malnutrition|Low weight for height|Obesity|Anaemia|Wasting is low weight for height, indicating acute malnutrition. Pakistan has one of the highest stunting rates in the region.
A trained person who assists childbirth in rural areas is:|Traditional birth attendant|Surgeon|Dentist|Pharmacist|Lady Health Workers (LHW) programme (1994) provides primary health care at community level in Pakistan.
The Lady Health Worker programme in Pakistan began in:|1994|1947|2005|2018|The National Programme for Family Planning and Primary Health Care started in 1994 and works in rural and peri-urban areas.
Which of the following is a function of a Child Protection Committee at the community level?|Identify risks and refer cases to the formal system|Collect taxes|Judge crimes|Run elections|Community-based child protection mechanisms link informal community actors to formal services.
In social casework, 'transference' means:|Client directs feelings about significant others toward the worker|Worker transfers files|Moving clients|Changing offices|Countertransference is the worker's emotional response to the client; supervision helps manage it.
Which statement about 'home visits' by a social worker is TRUE?|They provide direct observation of the child's environment|They are useless|They must be secret from the family|They replace court orders|Home visits help in assessment, monitoring and building trust. Safety of the worker should also be planned.
When a child cannot be returned to family and no kin are available, the next preferred option is:|Foster or family-type alternative care|Street life|Child labour|Adult prison|Long-term institutional care should be a last resort.
'Case conference' is:|A meeting of professionals to discuss a child's case and plan|A sports event|An exam|A fundraising|Case conferences improve coordinated decision-making. Minutes should be recorded and confidentiality maintained.
A Child Protection Officer receives a report of an abused child in a madrasa. Which is the most appropriate approach?|Take it seriously, ensure safety and follow legal procedure without bias|Ignore due to religion|Mock the child|Hide the case|Child protection applies to all settings including schools, madrasas and homes. Procedures remain the same.
Which of the following is a protective factor in a school setting?|Caring teacher and a safe environment|Bullying culture|Harsh punishment|Isolation|Positive school climate and connectedness reduce risk behaviour.
'Resilience' in children means:|Ability to adapt and recover despite adversity|Total absence of stress|Weakness|Aggression|Protective factors such as a supportive adult, problem-solving skills and community support promote resilience.
Which of the following is NOT a sign of neglect?|Child consistently well-fed and appropriately dressed|Persistent hunger|Poor hygiene|Untreated medical problems|Neglect indicators include chronic hunger, inadequate clothing, absence from school and lack of supervision.
Munchausen syndrome by proxy involves:|Caregiver inducing illness in a child to gain attention|Child pretending sickness|Genetic illness|Infection|Also called fabricated or induced illness (FII), it is a form of child abuse.
'Shaken baby syndrome' is a form of:|Abusive head trauma|Infection|Malnutrition|Vitamin deficiency|Violent shaking can cause brain damage or death in infants. Prevention requires caregiver education about crying and stress.
Which one is a legal duty of a medical practitioner treating a suspected abused child?|Provide treatment and medico-legal documentation and report|Refuse treatment|Discharge quickly|Conceal findings|Medico-legal certificates (MLC) are essential evidence in abuse cases.
The doctrine of 'in the best interest' guides:|Decision making for children|Corporate tax|Elections|Customs|It appears in the UNCRC, in domestic law (e.g., Guardians and Wards Act) and in policy.
Which of the following is a 'worst form' of child labour in Pakistan?|Brick kiln, carpet weaving with bonded debts, and domestic work under abuse|Reading books|Assisting teacher|Playing cricket|Children in bonded labour are especially vulnerable. Action needs multi-sectoral cooperation.
'Peshgi' in Pakistan is:|Advance loan that often binds labourers in debt bondage|Gift|Salary|Tax|Debt bondage is outlawed by the Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act 1992.
What does 'NOC' stand for?|No Objection Certificate|National Office Code|Notice of Change|Non-official Copy|NOCs are required for NGOs and many procedures. Familiarity with office abbreviations is useful in public service exams.
'SOP' stands for:|Standard Operating Procedure|Social Operational Plan|State Official Paper|System of Principles|SOPs describe steps for handling cases consistently, e.g., rescue SOP, referral SOP.
An MoU is:|Memorandum of Understanding|Method of Use|Ministry of Union|Money of Utility|MoUs set out cooperation between organizations, e.g., Bureau and an NGO for services.
A 'stakeholder' in a child protection programme includes:|Children, families, government and NGOs|Only donors|Only staff|Only media|Stakeholder analysis identifies who is affected, who has influence and how to engage them.
Which of the following is a social work practice principle for dealing with parents?|Respect and engage them as partners unless unsafe|Blame them always|Exclude them always|Humiliate them|Working with families improves outcomes. Safety remains the priority.
'Gatekeeping' in child protection refers to:|Screening to ensure children enter care only when necessary|Guarding gates|Immigration control|Security staff|Gatekeeping prevents unnecessary separation by exploring family support options first.
'Kinship care' refers to:|Care by relatives or close family friends|Care in prison|Foreign adoption|Boarding school|It is the most common form of alternative care globally and in Pakistan.
A 'care leaver' is:|A young person who leaves the alternative care system on reaching adulthood|A doctor|An orphan only|A teacher|Care leavers need preparation for independent living: education, jobs, housing and support.
The Social Welfare Department's 'Darul Aman' is mainly for:|Women victims of violence and their children|Prisoners|Soldiers|Farmers|Shelters give temporary accommodation, counselling and legal aid.
'Mental Health Gap Action Programme (mhGAP)' is a programme of:|WHO|ILO|FAO|IMF|mhGAP aims to scale up services for mental, neurological and substance use disorders in low- and middle-income countries.
The standard first-aid priority when a child is brought in injured and abused is:|Stabilize the child's medical condition and ensure safety|Interview at length|Take photos for media|Call the abuser|Medical stabilization comes first; evidence collection and interviews follow without delay in a child-friendly manner.
Identification of the 'best interests' of a child in a complex case is best done by:|A multidisciplinary assessment considering the child's views, safety and needs|Taking a vote among staff|Following the loudest voice|Using luck|Best interests determination weighs the child's safety, identity, views, family ties, health and education and is documented and reviewed.
