The headquarters of the United Nations is in:|New York|Geneva|Paris|Vienna|UN HQ is in Manhattan. Major UN offices are also in Geneva, Vienna and Nairobi.

The UN Security Council has how many permanent members?|Five|Seven|Ten|Fifteen|The P5 are USA, UK, France, Russia and China, each with veto power. There are 10 non-permanent members elected for 2-year terms (15 in total).
The headquarters of the World Health Organization (WHO) is in:|Geneva|New York|Paris|Rome|WHO was founded on 7 April 1948. World Health Day is 7 April.

UNESCO's headquarters is in:|Paris|Rome|Vienna|Geneva|UNESCO (1945) promotes education, science and culture and designates World Heritage Sites.
FAO is headquartered in:|Rome|Geneva|Vienna|Nairobi|The Food and Agriculture Organization was founded in 1945. WFP and IFAD are also in Rome.
IAEA and OPEC are both based in:|Vienna|Geneva|Brussels|London|IAEA (1957) monitors nuclear safeguards; OPEC (1960) coordinates oil policy.
NATO's headquarters is in:|Brussels|Washington|Paris|London|NATO was founded in 1949 with the North Atlantic Treaty. The EU's main institutions are in Brussels too.
The secretariat of SAARC is located in:|Kathmandu|Islamabad|Dhaka|New Delhi|SAARC was founded in 1985 at Dhaka summit; it has 8 members including Afghanistan.

ASEAN headquarters is in:|Jakarta|Bangkok|Singapore|Manila|ASEAN was founded in 1967 with five members and now has ten.

Interpol has its headquarters in:|Lyon, France|Vienna|Brussels|London|The International Criminal Police Organization facilitates cross-border police cooperation.
The FATF, the global anti-money laundering watchdog, is headquartered in:|Paris|Geneva|New York|Rome|Pakistan was on its 'grey list' from 2018 until October 2022.

The world's smallest country by area is:|Vatican City|Monaco|San Marino|Liechtenstein|Vatican City covers about 0.44 sq km and has fewer than 1000 residents.
Which country is the most populous in the world?|India|China|USA|Indonesia|India overtook China in 2023. The world population reached 8 billion in November 2022.
The largest ocean in the world is:|Pacific Ocean|Atlantic Ocean|Indian Ocean|Arctic Ocean|It covers about one-third of the Earth's surface and contains the Mariana Trench, the deepest point (Challenger Deep).
The longest river in the world is generally considered to be:|Nile|Amazon|Yangtze|Mississippi|The Nile flows north through 11 countries. Some measurements suggest the Amazon is longer.
The tallest mountain above sea level is:|Mount Everest (8,849 m)|K2|Kangchenjunga|Makalu|Everest lies on the Nepal–Tibet border. K2 is the second highest.
The largest hot desert in the world is:|Sahara|Gobi|Thar|Kalahari|The Sahara in North Africa covers about 9 million sq km. Antarctica is the largest desert overall.
The largest lake in the world by area is:|Caspian Sea|Lake Superior|Lake Victoria|Lake Baikal|Lake Baikal in Russia is the deepest and holds the largest volume of fresh water.
The capital of Australia is:|Canberra|Sydney|Melbourne|Perth|Canberra was purpose-built as a compromise between Sydney and Melbourne.
The capital of Turkey is:|Ankara|Istanbul|Izmir|Bursa|Istanbul is the largest city. Ankara became capital in 1923 under Atatürk.
The capital of Canada is:|Ottawa|Toronto|Vancouver|Montreal|Ottawa lies in Ontario near the Quebec border.
The capital of Brazil is:|Brasília|Rio de Janeiro|São Paulo|Salvador|Brasília was inaugurated in 1960.
The capital of Switzerland is:|Bern|Zurich|Geneva|Basel|Geneva is a global diplomatic city but Bern is the federal capital.
The capital of Myanmar is:|Naypyidaw|Yangon|Mandalay|Bago|The capital shifted from Yangon in 2005–06.
The capital of Kazakhstan is:|Astana|Almaty|Tashkent|Bishkek|Renamed Nur-Sultan in 2019 and back to Astana in 2022.
The capital of Sri Lanka is:|Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte|Colombo|Kandy|Galle|Colombo is the commercial capital while Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte is the legislative capital.
The capital of Saudi Arabia is:|Riyadh|Jeddah|Makkah|Madinah|Jeddah is the commercial hub, Makkah and Madinah are holy cities.
The capital of the UAE is:|Abu Dhabi|Dubai|Sharjah|Ajman|The UAE is a federation of seven emirates.
The capital of Egypt is:|Cairo|Alexandria|Luxor|Giza|Egypt is home to the pyramids at Giza and the Suez Canal.
The capital of Iran is:|Tehran|Isfahan|Shiraz|Tabriz|Tehran has been the capital since 1789.
