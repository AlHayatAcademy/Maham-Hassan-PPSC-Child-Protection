# Test 12 - Constitution, governance and public administration
Article 1 of the Constitution of Pakistan declares Pakistan as a:|Federal Republic|Unitary state|Confederation|Monarchy|Article 1 says Pakistan shall be a Federal Republic to be known as the Islamic Republic of Pakistan.
Article 2 of the Constitution states that the State religion is:|Islam|Secularism|Federalism|Democracy|Article 2A (inserted by the Eighth Amendment, 1985) makes the Objectives Resolution a substantive part of the Constitution.
Article 6 of the Constitution deals with:|High treason|Right to life|Education|Federal List|Subverting or abrogating the Constitution by force is high treason. Punishment is death or life imprisonment under the High Treason (Punishment) Act 1973.
Article 8 says that any law inconsistent with Fundamental Rights is:|Void|Valid|Temporary|Optional|Fundamental Rights are superior to ordinary laws. Courts can strike down such laws.
Article 9 guarantees:|Security of person (life and liberty)|Freedom of speech|Education|Property|No person shall be deprived of life or liberty save in accordance with law. The Supreme Court has expanded it to include a clean environment and livelihood.
Article 19 guarantees:|Freedom of speech|Freedom of religion|Right to property|Right to dignity|It is subject to reasonable restrictions in the interest of Islam and security. Article 19A (right to information) was added in 2010.
Freedom of religion is protected under Article:|20|18|22|25|Art. 20 gives every citizen the right to profess, practice and propagate religion, subject to law, public order and morality.
Equality of citizens before law is in Article:|25|27|9|14|Art. 25: all citizens are equal before law and entitled to equal protection of law; no discrimination on the basis of sex alone.
Article 27 deals with:|Safeguard against discrimination in services|Freedom of movement|Property rights|Elections|It prohibits discrimination in public employment on grounds of race, religion, caste, sex, residence or place of birth.
Which Article provides for the right to information?|19A|10A|25A|9A|Right to information (2010) is available to all citizens in matters of public importance subject to regulation and reasonable restrictions.
Who elects the President of Pakistan?|Electoral college comprising Senate, National Assembly and Provincial Assemblies|Direct popular vote|Supreme Court|Prime Minister|The term is five years. The President must be a Muslim, a citizen and at least 45 years of age.
The minimum age to become President of Pakistan is:|45 years|35 years|40 years|25 years|Qualifications are in Article 41(2). Minimum age to be a member of the National Assembly is 25 and for the Senate 30.
The minimum age for membership of the Senate is:|30 years|25 years|35 years|40 years|The Senate has 96 members and is a permanent House: it cannot be dissolved, and half of the members retire every three years.
The term of the National Assembly is:|5 years|4 years|6 years|3 years|Under Article 52 the National Assembly continues for five years from the first meeting unless sooner dissolved.
The Senate of Pakistan has how many members?|96|100|104|88|Members were increased from 104 to 96 after the FATA merger (25th Amendment 2018). Each province has 23 members, ICT 4.
The National Assembly of Pakistan has how many total seats?|336 (266 general, 60 women, 10 minorities)|342|272|300|After the 25th Amendment, general seats were reduced from 272 to 266 following the merger of FATA with KP.
The Prime Minister is elected by:|Members of the National Assembly|Senate|Supreme Court|President directly|The President invites the member likely to command the confidence of a majority in the National Assembly (Article 91).
Under Article 91, the PM must be a:|Muslim member of the National Assembly|Judge|Senator only|Army officer|Following the 18th Amendment, the PM is the chief executive and the head of the Cabinet.
The 18th Constitutional Amendment (2010):|Devolved powers to provinces and abolished the Concurrent List|Increased president's powers|Introduced martial law|Created Senate|It also renamed NWFP as Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, added Article 25A and Article 10A and 19A, and removed the President's power to dissolve the National Assembly (Article 58(2)(b)).
The 8th Amendment (1985) inserted Article ___ giving the President power to dissolve the National Assembly.|58(2)(b)|62|63|184|The 13th Amendment (1997) removed it, the 17th (2003) restored it and the 18th Amendment finally removed it.
Which Amendment merged FATA with Khyber Pakhtunkhwa?|25th Amendment (2018)|18th Amendment|21st Amendment|8th Amendment|FATA's merger ended the Frontier Crimes Regulation (FCR) and extended jurisdiction of courts to the area.
Article 140A of the Constitution requires provinces to:|Establish local government systems|Establish courts|Hold elections|Collect Zakat|Added by the 18th Amendment: each province shall establish local government and devolve political, administrative and financial responsibility to local government institutions.
The National Finance Commission (NFC) is constituted under Article:|160|184|199|209|NFC distributes the federal divisible pool between federation and provinces. The 7th NFC Award was announced in 2010.
Under the 7th NFC Award, the share of provinces in the divisible pool is:|57.5 percent|50 percent|40 percent|70 percent|Punjab got 51.74%, Sindh 24.55%, KP 14.62% and Balochistan 9.09% of the provincial share (with Balochistan receiving an additional grant).
The superior courts of Pakistan include:|Supreme Court, Federal Shariat Court and High Courts|Only District courts|Only Tribunals|Only Magistrate courts|The Federal Shariat Court was established in 1980 to examine laws for repugnancy to Islam.
Original jurisdiction of the Supreme Court to enforce Fundamental Rights is under Article:|184(3)|199|212|203D|It allows the Court to take up matters of public importance concerning enforcement of fundamental rights.
The Supreme Judicial Council inquires into misconduct of judges under Article:|209|184|199|175A|The Council consists of the Chief Justice and senior judges. Judges can be removed only through this procedure.
Who administers the oath to the Prime Minister?|President|Chief Justice|Speaker|Governor|The President administers the oath. The Chief Justice administers the oath to the President.
The Chief Election Commissioner is appointed under Article:|213|90|140|240|Article 218 establishes the Election Commission of Pakistan (ECP). The CEC is appointed for three years.
The Auditor General of Pakistan is appointed under Article:|168|184|199|209|He audits federal and provincial government accounts and reports to the President/Governor, which are laid before the PAC.
The Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC) conducts the:|CSS Competitive Examination|PPSC exams|School exams|Matric exams|FPSC recruits for BPS-16 and above federal posts. The Punjab Public Service Commission (PPSC) conducts recruitment for provincial posts like Child Protection Officer.
The Constitution makes provision for the Public Service Commission under Article:|242|240|243|245|Article 242 deals with Public Service Commissions (federal and provincial). Article 240 deals with appointments to the service of Pakistan.
Which Article of the Constitution places the Armed Forces of Pakistan under the control of the Federal Government?|243|245|184|209|Article 243 deals with command of the Armed Forces. Article 245 provides for their functions.
The head of a province is the:|Governor|Chief Minister|Chief Justice|Speaker|The Governor is appointed by the President on the advice of the Prime Minister. The Chief Minister is the chief executive.
The Punjab Provincial Assembly has total seats:|371|297|330|250|Total is 371 of which 297 are general seats, 66 reserved for women and 8 for non-Muslims.
The Governor of a province may promulgate an Ordinance under Article:|128|89|58|91|Article 89 allows the President to issue ordinances. Ordinances in provinces lapse after 90 days unless extended by the assembly.
Money Bill is introduced in:|National Assembly only|Senate only|Either house|Supreme Court|Money Bills (Article 73) originate in the National Assembly; the Senate can only make recommendations within 14 days.
The Punjab Civil Servants Act was passed in:|1974|1947|1962|2001|It governs the terms and conditions of service of civil servants in Punjab. Appointments and promotions are by rules.
The Punjab Employees Efficiency, Discipline and Accountability Act (PEEDA) was passed in:|2006|1974|1990|2020|It provides disciplinary action for misconduct, inefficiency and corruption among government employees.
Pakistan's fiscal year runs from:|1 July to 30 June|1 January to 31 December|1 April to 31 March|1 October to 30 September|The federal budget is presented in June each year by the Finance Minister in the National Assembly.
BPS stands for:|Basic Pay Scale|Budget Planning System|Basic Public Service|Banking Pay Slab|Pakistan has Basic Pay Scales 1–22. BPS-17 and above are officer-level posts such as Child Protection Officer (BPS-17).
Who is considered the 'father of public administration' for his 1887 essay 'The Study of Administration'?|Woodrow Wilson|Max Weber|Henri Fayol|F.W. Taylor|Wilson argued for separating politics from administration. Weber's model of bureaucracy features hierarchy, rules and merit.
Max Weber's ideal-type bureaucracy has all EXCEPT:|Nepotism|Hierarchy|Written rules|Merit-based appointment|Bureaucracy uses impersonality and technical competence. Nepotism contradicts merit.
F.W. Taylor is known as the father of:|Scientific management|Human relations|Bureaucracy|Administration|Taylor's 'Principles of Scientific Management' (1911) emphasized time and motion studies.
The Hawthorne studies, a base of the human relations approach, were led by:|Elton Mayo|Taylor|Fayol|Weber|They showed social factors and attention to workers affect productivity.
Citizens can seek public information in Punjab under the:|Punjab Transparency and Right to Information Act 2013|Arms Act|Punjab Food Act|Land Act|The Act created the Punjab Information Commission to hear appeals.
The Punjab Right to Public Services Act was passed in:|2018|1990|2005|2022|It guarantees delivery of notified public services within a fixed time, and the Punjab Service Delivery commission monitors.
The Wafaqi Mohtasib (Federal Ombudsman) institution was established in:|1983|1947|2000|2010|Mohtasib investigates complaints of maladministration against federal agencies. Punjab has its own Mohtasib set up in 1996.

NADRA was formed in:|2000|1947|1971|2013|The National Database and Registration Authority was set up under the NADRA Ordinance 2000 and issues CNIC and B-Form.
