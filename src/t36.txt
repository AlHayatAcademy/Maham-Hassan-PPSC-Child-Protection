# Test 36 - English III: grammar - tenses, voice, narration, prepositions, articles and agreement
Fill in the blank: He ___ to Lahore yesterday.|went|goes|gone|going|The past simple is needed with 'yesterday'.
Fill in the blank: I have been living here ___ 2015.|since|for|from|at|'Since' is used with a point in time; 'for' with a period.
Fill in the blank: I have been living here ___ five years.|for|since|from|by|'For' indicates duration.
Fill in the blank: Each of the girls ___ a prize.|received|receive|were receiving|have received|'Each' is singular.
Fill in the blank: Ten kilometres ___ a long distance.|is|are|were|have been|Distances are treated as a single unit.
Fill in the blank: He is senior ___ me.|to|than|from|of|Senior, junior, superior and inferior take 'to'.
Fill in the blank: She is good ___ mathematics.|at|in|on|with|'Good at' for skills.
Fill in the blank: He is fond ___ music.|of|for|in|with|'Fond of' is the correct collocation.
Fill in the blank: She is afraid ___ snakes.|of|from|with|by|'Afraid of'.
Fill in the blank: The book is ___ the table.|on|in|at|by|'On' for surface contact.
Fill in the blank: He died ___ cancer.|of|from|by|with|'Died of' for a disease; 'died by' a weapon.
Fill in the blank: She was born ___ 1995.|in|on|at|by|'In' with years; 'on' with dates; 'at' with time.
Fill in the blank: The meeting is ___ 10 a.m.|at|in|on|by|Use 'at' for clock time.
Fill in the blank: We have a meeting ___ Monday.|on|in|at|by|Use 'on' with days.
Fill in the blank: He apologised ___ his mistake.|for|of|on|at|'Apologise for'.
Fill in the blank: I prefer tea ___ coffee.|to|than|from|over|'Prefer ... to'.
Fill in the blank: He insisted ___ paying the bill.|on|to|for|at|'Insist on'.
Fill in the blank: She is married ___ a doctor.|to|with|by|from|'Married to'.
Fill in the blank: ___ umbrella is useful in the rain.|An|A|The an|Some|Use 'an' before a vowel sound.
Fill in the blank: He is ___ honest man.|an|a|the|no article|'Honest' begins with a silent h, so it uses 'an'.
Fill in the blank: She is ___ European student.|a|an|the|no article|'European' begins with a /j/ sound, so 'a'.
Fill in the blank: He is ___ M.A. in English.|an|a|the|no article|'M' is pronounced 'em', a vowel sound.
Fill in the blank: ___ Sun rises in the east.|The|A|An|No article|Unique objects take 'the'.
Change into the passive voice: 'He writes a letter.'|A letter is written by him.|A letter was written by him.|A letter is being written by him.|A letter has been written by him.|Simple present active becomes 'is/are + past participle'.
Change into the passive voice: 'They are playing football.'|Football is being played by them.|Football is played by them.|Football was being played by them.|Football has been played by them.|Present continuous becomes 'is/are being + past participle'.
Change into the passive voice: 'She has completed the work.'|The work has been completed by her.|The work was completed by her.|The work is completed by her.|The work had been completed by her.|Present perfect becomes 'has/have been + past participle'.
Change into the passive voice: 'Open the door.'|Let the door be opened.|The door is opened.|The door was opened.|The door be opened.|Imperative sentences use 'Let ... be + past participle'.
Change into active voice: 'The poem was written by Iqbal.'|Iqbal wrote the poem.|Iqbal writes the poem.|Iqbal has written the poem.|Iqbal is writing the poem.|Simple past passive becomes simple past active.
Change into indirect speech: She said, 'I will come tomorrow.'|She said that she would come the next day.|She said that she will come tomorrow.|She said that she would come tomorrow.|She said that I would come the next day.|'Will' becomes 'would' and 'tomorrow' becomes 'the next day'.
Change into indirect speech: He said to me, 'What is your name?'|He asked me what my name was.|He asked me what is my name.|He said me what my name was.|He asked me that what my name was.|Wh- questions do not use 'that' in reported speech.
Change into indirect speech: She said, 'Alas! I am ruined.'|She exclaimed with sorrow that she was ruined.|She said that alas she was ruined.|She told alas she is ruined.|She exclaimed that she is ruined.|'Alas' shows sorrow.
The plural of 'child' is:|Children|Childs|Childes|Childrens|It is an irregular plural.
The plural of 'criterion' is:|Criteria|Criterions|Criterias|Criterium|Like 'phenomenon', which is 'phenomena'.
The plural of 'mother-in-law' is:|Mothers-in-law|Mother-in-laws|Mothers-in-laws|Mother-in-law's|The main noun takes the plural form.
The comparative of 'good' is:|Better|Gooder|More good|Best|Best is the superlative.
The superlative of 'bad' is:|Worst|Baddest|Worse|Most bad|Worse is the comparative.
Choose the correct spelling:|Accommodation|Acommodation|Accomodation|Acomodation|The word has double 'c' and double 'm'.
Fill in the blank: If I ___ you, I would apologise.|were|was|am|be|The subjunctive 'were' is used in unreal conditions.
Fill in the blank: She sings ___ than her sister.|better|best|more good|gooder|Comparative of 'well' is 'better'.
Fill in the blank: He was ___ tired to walk.|too|very|so that|enough|'Too ... to' shows excess.
Fill in the blank: Please wait ___ I come back.|until|since|for|during|'Until' marks the end of a period.
Fill in the blank: She has lived here ___ ten years.|for|since|from|by|'For' with a length of time.
Fill in the blank: He is the ___ student in the class.|tallest|taller|more tall|most taller|Superlative form of 'tall'.
Fill in the blank: I look forward ___ you.|to meeting|to meet|for meeting|at meeting|'Look forward to' takes the gerund.
Fill in the blank: She is interested ___ painting.|in|at|on|for|'Interested in'.
Fill in the blank: He has been absent ___ Monday.|since|for|from|by|'Since' with a point in time.
Fill in the blank: I am looking ___ my keys.|for|at|on|up|'Look for' means to search.
Fill in the blank: They are proud ___ their son.|of|on|about|for|'Proud of'.
Fill in the blank: He is accused ___ theft.|of|for|with|by|'Accused of'.
Fill in the blank: The sun ___ in the east.|rises|rise|rose|rising|A universal truth takes the simple present.
